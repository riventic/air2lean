[PR135](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/135): spans from a real patched export (`tests/roadmap/counterexamples/native`): the off-by-one `sumUpTo` counterexample carries its declaration span and the overflowing `scale` counterexample the `mul_safe` statement span at the line and column of `*`; `--zig-source` adds a Zig-side replay (`replay.zig_native`: stock Zig builds the function with the shared harness, runs the violating input in a child process and compares with the Lean outcome by the differential rule), and a violation that is itself an illegal or model-panic outcome lists the sites of that failure; a `lean-eval` timeout kills the whole process group. Remaining: the Zig-side replay covers sequential integer/`bool` functions only; goal search covers plain `Zig.Result` functions over `BitVec`/`Bool`; spans are approximate for inlined or std code.
