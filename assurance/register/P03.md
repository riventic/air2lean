row: [PR120](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/120), [PR128](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/128), [PR207](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/207), [PR222](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/222): generated loops are proved in the full-state logic with the loop rules of ZigLean/Sep/Full/Loop.lean (`FTotalTriple.loop_measure`, `loop_ghost`, `loop_dispatch`, `FTriple.loop`), each body's step walked by `fsym`; a proved loop is a rule, so nested loops compose (a translated nested loop proved total with retained AIR and a byte-identical retranslation check); range conversions are `fsym` step rules for checked arithmetic and unsigned `@intCast` with its arithmetic solver `fsym_arith`, and ZigLean/Range.lean keeps the sum bounds; a linked-list queue walk is proved total as an instance of Proofs/Lists/Linked.lean without unfolding memory internals; generic recursive-group contracts (`FTotalTriple.rec_group`, PR433/PR557) walk self and mutual recursion with `fsym`, with parity (PR597), all-input gcd (PR613) and bounded factorial (PR620) as instances. `Proofs/Pointers/Sep.lean` now proves the current generated memory-backed `addDown` with the exact accumulator result, current-thread/stack-depth restoration, explicit unmetered mode, nonoverflow and room for all n+1 frames, plus a budgeted start-memory witness. Side conditions `fsym` cannot prove remain visible goals. Loops in a thread have the same rules (`ZigLean/Sep/Full/ThreadLoop.lean`). The acceptance's two clauses are evidenced: `tests/roadmap/loop-tactics/Queue.lean` proves `Lists.Queue.sum_total` (an `FTotalTriple` over the generated 0.17.1 `Lists.Open.sum`) as an instance of `Lists.Linked.sum_total`, with only the standard axioms (`#guard_msgs` on `#print axioms`); the walk's residuals are reported (`report.residuals`, docs/proof-automation.md "Machine-readable residual reports"; `SepAuto/Infer.lean`, `SepAuto/ListWalk.lean`); and the designated mutants `queue-overflow-premise-dropped` and `queue-sum-off-by-one` are killed (`assurance/mutation-kills.json`). Remaining (close-out review 2026-11-26), all recorded as open in the engine ledger against this requirement: (1) AIR/native acceptance of memory-backed recursion: `Certificate.fragment` still refuses recursive memory functions because the AIR reference has no stack semantics, so `addDown_total` is a proof of the generated `MemM` code only (STK-01; ledger `recursive-group-total`, a separately scheduled feature, owner: coordinator); (2) the recursive-group walk's open matcher debt: the hand congruence after `fsym` in `addDown_group`, and the compatibility projections `isEven_isOdd_aux`, `gcd_spec_aux` and `fact_ok_aux`, whose retirement needs a maintainer decision (ledger `recursive-group-total`); (3) entry-tick/producer integration of recursive cliques (ledger `canonical-tick-certification`, PR510, a draft that is not READY). Scope, not remaining: invariants and measures are the client's (inference is P09); total correctness of a loop whose exit waits on another thread is liveness (C13); the unmetered generated-model proof gives no cost or native-stack bound.
