row: Design and recommendation in [docs/research/F03.md](docs/research/F03.md): a deterministic executable wrapper per `*_optimized` op (irreducible to `fsym`, `.illegal` for a NaN/inf operand or result) and a proof-side real-valued envelope `Permitted e` over ghost expressions of the non-flagged leaves, with `@[fsym]` rules and a per-use `observe`; nnan/ninf are poison (LLVM), reassoc/contract/arcp act on connected regions, so a per-operation relation is unsound. Evidence: `tests/roadmap/fast-math` (native strict/fast probe pairs, 0.14.1 to 0.17.0, aarch64-macos and x86_64-linux in Docker, `check.py`); nine cases diverge in every release record, `sum_cancel` is `62.0` strict and `60.0` fast on aarch64 only. Finding: Zig 0.14.1 folds `x + 0.0` in strict mode (draft `docs/upstream/float-add-zero-fold.md`). Remaining: the whole implementation; the normalizer still rejects every `*_optimized` tag; premises MTH-09/MTH-10 to be assigned.
