title: Endianness support
status: partial
row: [PR129](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/129): byte order is a parameter (`Zig.ByteOrder`, ZigLean/Endian.lean) of integer, float, slice-length, enum, packed backing-integer, bit-pointer host and whole-byte vector-lane encodings; every `.little` definition is the existing model by `rfl` (little-endian translations are byte-identical) and ZigLean/EndianLemmas.lean proves round trips at both orders. The translator accepts a schema-12 s390x-linux profile (`abi64-be-v1`, premise PRF-05, LLVM backend only) and fails closed on atomics, std models, external models, packed unions, f80, non-byte or bool/pointer vector lanes, `@tagName`/`@errorName`, inline asm and byte pointers into packed structs. One source exported for s390x-linux and x86_64-linux translates, its byte-level facts are proved for both, and the native program under qemu s390x and on x86_64 matches the model (CI). Batch D (PR "Roadmap batch D: L08 L07 T03 L04"): big-endian atomics and futexes are the little-endian ops conjugated by `byteSwap` (`ZigLean/Conc/Endian.lean`, `…COf .big`; each `…COf .little` is the existing op by `rfl`; `atomicRmwFnAt`), admitted for representations of at most 8 bits or a whole number of bytes; the std allocator, thread and OS models are admitted on big-endian profiles; a second big-endian target, mips64-linux (n64, `mips64r2`), is qualified: `big_endian.zig` translates to the s390x text, and its native output under qemu equals the model's and s390x's; `concurrency.zig` (atomic stores read through bytes, plain bytes read atomically, RMW carry, 64-bit `cmpxchg`, signed `Max`, two threads, a heap word, a futex wait whose expected value is the swapped word) exported by Zig 0.17.1 and 0.15.2 for s390x, mips64 and x86_64 gives the native output under 24 scheduler oracles each. Zig 0.17.1 (2026-11-24): `big_endian.zig` and `reject.zig` are exported by 0.17.1 and 0.16.0 for s390x, mips64 and x86_64 (`tests/roadmap/big-endian/BigEndian/<version>/<target>`; 0.17 has no `extern struct` `@bitCast`, so `externToBytes` reads its bytes through a pointer there); on 0.17.1 a `@bitCast` to or from bytes is the logical bit order and gives the same bytes on both orders, natively and in the model, while memory views keep the target's order; the model's 115 lines equal the native output of each version on all three targets (`check.sh`, `check.sh --native <version>`), the byte-level facts are proved for both versions (`BigEndian.Proofs`), and every `reject.zig` rejection holds on both. Remaining: negative tests and designated mutants (Q02); packed unions, `f80`, non-byte or `bool`/pointer vector lanes, packed structs with float fields, `@tagName`/`@errorName`, inline asm, byte pointers into packed structs, external models and non-byte atomic widths stay rejected on big-endian profiles; other big-endian targets (powerpc64, sparc64; no runner here) and other backends.
