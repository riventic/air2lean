title: Loop switch and switch dispatch
status: complete
row: Completed (2026-11-24, 0.17.1 qualification). [PR124](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/124): nested legal exits (two-level break, outer continue, inner return, inner loop result as outer selector, memory captures) translate; nested dispatches to an enclosing ordinary loop, a sibling loop-switch, itself, a non-control instruction or an absent ID, and wrong-kind/missing br/repeat targets are rejected; a loop invariant and termination measure are proved for the generated nested countdown machine (`Zig.loop_spec`). [PR129](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/129), [PR222](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/222): a generated labeled-switch dispatch loop is proved by the full-state loop rule `FTotalTriple.loop_dispatch` (ZigLean/Sep/Full/Loop.lean) with a state-indexed invariant and a measure combining the data measure with a rank of the state, one `fsym` walk per state; a real export of a tokenizer state machine is proved total and correct against a Lean reference this way; negative controls reject wrong invariants and flat measures. Zig 0.17.1 (the `loop_switch_br`/`switch_dispatch` contract of `src/Air.zig` is unchanged): `tests/roadmap/dispatch/check.sh` accepts 0.17.1 (the default) besides 0.16.0, 0.15.2 and 0.14.1 and passes on it (stock native run, fresh patched export with nested dispatch targets, translation, generated value checks, synthetic machines and mutants); `tests/roadmap/dispatch-templates/check.sh` passes on 0.17.1 and 0.16.0 (the tokenizer proof over the fresh translation, whose renumbered loop is the same, and the real-AIR dispatches to a block, an absent ID and itself rejected as "not an enclosing loop-switch"). Follow-up: per-client invariants and measures (P09); C `goto` state machines (X01; translate-c demotes them); unrestricted control flow stays rejected.
