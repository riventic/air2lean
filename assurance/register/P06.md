row: Step counts as a resource of the full-state logic: the translator puts a `Zig.tick` at every loop-body run and recursive entry ([PR562](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/562)), a step needs a credit or the plain mode, and ZigLean/Sep/Full/Cost.lean ([PR568](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/568)) reads exact counts off `FTotalTriple` (`ticks_le`/`ticks_ge`/`ticks_eq`), makes bounds tight (`not_fewer_credits`) and counts loops (`loop_exact`); `Lists.Linked.sum_exact` (`xs.length + 1` credits) is tight and exact for the generated `sum` (Proofs/Lists/Bounded.lean). The legacy allocation counts of the hand-written heap (PR122: `Mem.liveHeap`, `push_cost`, `append_capacity`) are retired with it. Queue-operation bound: for the translated 0.17.1 ring buffer of tests/roadmap/queue-adt, and for every implementation of the queue contract `Zig.Full.QueueImpl`, `pushAll_exact` proves that under the explicit capacity premise `xs.length + ys.length ≤ C` the generated `pushAll` pushes every item for `ys.length + 1` credits and as many receipts (`pushAll_steps`: exactly that many steps of a run; `pushAll_not_within`: tight; a mutant with the premise weakened fails). Counts are model counts (SEM-05), not time or native memory. The acceptance sentence is met by that theorem; the row stays partial until the contract structure `QueueImpl` is approved in the proof-framework allow-list. Remaining beyond it: allocation counts over a translated allocator (retained allocations), failing/diverging runs, concurrent cost.
