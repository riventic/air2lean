row: Completed (2026-11-25, `QueueImpl` approved in the proof-framework allow-list): step counts are a resource of the full-state logic: the translator puts a `Zig.tick` at every loop-body run and recursive entry ([PR562](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/562)), a step needs a credit or the plain mode, and ZigLean/Sep/Full/Cost.lean ([PR568](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/568)) reads exact counts off `FTotalTriple` (`ticks_le`/`ticks_ge`/`ticks_eq`), makes bounds tight (`not_fewer_credits`) and counts loops (`loop_exact`); `Lists.Linked.sum_exact` (`xs.length + 1` credits) is tight and exact for the generated `sum` (Proofs/Lists/Bounded.lean). Queue-operation bounds: the queue contract `Zig.Full.QueueImpl` declares each operation's exact step count as a function of the abstract queue; the translated 0.17.1 shifting array of tests/roadmap/queue-adt proves its `pop` in exactly `xs.length` steps (`Shift.pop_spec`, its loop by `loop_exact`; a mutant one credit short fails), a rotation costs the declared counts of its `pop` and `push` for every implementation (`rotate_spec`, `rotateShift_spec`), and under the explicit capacity premise `xs.length + ys.length ≤ C` the generated `pushAll` pushes every item for `ys.length + 1` credits and as many receipts for every implementation whose `push` runs no step (`pushAll_exact`, `pushAll_steps`: exactly that many steps of a run; `pushAll_not_within`: tight; a mutant with the premise weakened fails). Counts are model counts (SEM-05), not time or native memory. Beyond the acceptance: a loop that calls an operation of a nonzero declared count (engine ledger T1: `credits` atoms are not split at a call), allocation counts over a translated allocator (retained allocations), failing/diverging runs, concurrent cost.
