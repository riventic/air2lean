id: M01
title: Multiple allocator identities and policies
status: partial
row: [PR124](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/124): blocks record their owning allocator (`.owned a`); arena and fixed-buffer policies follow the Zig 0.16 sources; cross-allocator free/destroy/remap is `.illegal`; reset/deinit end exactly the arena's blocks; a request-scoped arena session client is proved for every failure policy. Remaining: the translator does not route `std.heap` arena/fixed-buffer calls (the client is hand-written), owned blocks get fresh model addresses, growing remap fails, and custom allocators are not modelled. [PR139](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/139): allocators proved from their translated Zig code down to `posix.mmap`/`munmap`/`mremap` only (`--allocator-model translated`, premise OSM-01; `docs/allocator-model.md`). The translated `std.heap.FixedBufferAllocator` satisfies the generic `AllocSpec` (`FBA.allocSpec`, total) and the full-state `FAllocSpec` (`FBA.fallocSpec`), and a client is proved through the translated `std.mem.Allocator` wrappers (`tests/roadmap/alloc-fba`). The translated `std.heap.PageAllocator`: `free`/`resize`/`remap` are total triples on x86_64-linux and aarch64-macos (`PageSpec`, `PageSpecMacos`); `alloc` is proved on x86_64-linux for every alignment, as partial correctness (`PageAlloc.fallocSpec : FAllocSpec FLogic.partial`; `tests/roadmap/alloc-translated`). O5 is fixed in the OS model (OSM-01): `mmap`/`mremap` fail with `ENOMEM` above the target's user address space (`Os.Target.addrLimit`), so `alignPointer` cannot overflow and `map`'s prefix and tail `munmap`s are proved. Remaining for milestone 1: total correctness of `alloc`, `alloc` on aarch64-macos, the FBA client port onto the full-state spec, the unmapped prefix of a partly live mapping is never reused by the placement, and removing the hand-written models (`.owned`, the fixed buffer of `ZigLean/Mem/Owned.lean`, the `AllocPolicy` variants; plan in `docs/allocator-model.md`). Milestone 2 (the arena landing, `docs/alloc-arena.md`): `std.heap.ArenaAllocator` (Zig 0.16.0, lock-free) is translated from its AIR with its child allocator (an indirect call through `child_allocator.vtable`, read as const-global knowledge); `free`, `resize` and `remap` of the stock arena (x86_64-linux, aarch64-macos) and of a patched arena are proved against `FAllocSpec`; the stock arena satisfies `FAllocSpec`'s `alloc` for no child (obstructions O-A and O-E, kernel-checked; upstream patch draft). Remaining for milestone 2: `alloc` and `reset` of the patched arena.
