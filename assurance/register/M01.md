id: M01
title: Multiple allocator identities and policies
status: partial
row: [PR124](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/124): blocks record their owning allocator (`.owned a`); arena and fixed-buffer policies follow the Zig 0.16 sources; cross-allocator free/destroy/remap is `.illegal`; reset/deinit end exactly the arena's blocks; a request-scoped arena session client was proved for every failure policy (these proofs over the hand-written model are retired, and the model itself is deleted, see below). Remaining: custom allocators are not modelled. [PR139](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/139): allocators proved from their translated Zig code down to `posix.mmap`/`munmap`/`mremap` only (`--allocator-model translated`, premise OSM-01; `docs/allocator-model.md`). The translated `std.heap.FixedBufferAllocator` satisfies the full-state `FAllocSpec` (`FBA.fallocSpec`, total) as the second reset ledger (`FBA.ledger`: its fee is a part of the epoch token; `FBA.freset_spec` takes the whole token of epoch `e` and gives back that of `e + 1` with the whole buffer free again; `FBA.fown_init` starts the ledger), and a client is proved through the translated `std.mem.Allocator` wrappers' contracts over it, walked by `fsym` (`Client.client_spec`, and from the program's start memory `Client.client_start`; the legacy bridge and client are retired, L6; `tests/roadmap/alloc-fba`). Zig 0.17.1 and 0.16.0: the retained translation is 0.17.1's, and the whole library (the `FBA.fallocSpec` proof, the wrapper and client contracts) is built and audited over it and again over 0.16.0's in a check tree (`alloc-fba/check.sh`); the entries translate to the same code in both. The translated `std.heap.PageAllocator`: `free`/`resize`/`remap` are total triples on x86_64-linux and aarch64-macos (`PageSpec`, `PageSpecMacos`); `alloc` is proved on x86_64-linux and aarch64-macos for alignments up to a page (`k ≤ 12`, `k ≤ 14`), as partial correctness, over one allocator state for every page size (`PageTok.hown`; `PageAlloc.fallocSpec`, `PageAllocMacos.fallocSpec : FAllocSpec FLogic.partial`, each with its creation triple from the start memory's null hint word; `tests/roadmap/alloc-translated`). On both targets the four entries are also thread triples and the allocator meets the concurrent contract as an environment of an open `std.mem.Allocator` (`PageThread.cAllocSpec`, `PageThreadMacos.cAllocSpec : CAllocSpec (CVTable.ofRaw vt) c ainv`, with `cAllocSpec.creation`; the same alignments, partial correctness; OSM-01 is the only premise, the hint word being the allocator's only shared state). O5 is fixed in the OS model (OSM-01): `mmap`/`mremap` fail with `ENOMEM` above the target's user address space (`Os.Target.addrLimit`), so `alignPointer` cannot overflow; the runs through `map`'s prefix and tail `munmap`s are kernel-checked (`alloc_top`, `alloc_edge`), but the proof of `alloc` for alignments above a page is open (an earlier register text said "every alignment": that was the hand-walked legacy proof, removed 2026-10-12; engine-ledger row `page-alloc-large-align`). Remaining for milestone 1: total correctness of `alloc` (blocked on a framework decision: no judgment states the total correctness of a `ConcM` call and `fsym` does not walk its in-thread reading, engine-ledger row `conc-call-total`), `alloc` for alignments above a page, the unmapped prefix of a partly live mapping is never reused by the placement, and removing the remaining hand-written models (the `AllocPolicy` variants and the std allocator; plan in `docs/allocator-model.md`, steps 3 to 5). Steps 1 and 2 of that plan are done: the hand-written fixed buffer and arena (`ZigLean/Mem/Owned.lean`, `OwnedPolicy`, `OwnedAlloc`, `AllocRef`, `BlockKind.owned`, `Mem.allocators`), their executable regressions (`tests/roadmap/allocator-identity`, the arena case of `tests/roadmap/address-reuse`) and premise ALC-07 are deleted, replaced by the translated `FixedBufferAllocator` above and the translated `ArenaAllocator` below (no theorem listed ALC-07). Milestone 2 (the arena landing, `docs/alloc-arena.md`): `std.heap.ArenaAllocator` (Zig 0.16.0, lock-free) is translated from its AIR with its child allocator (an indirect call through `child_allocator.vtable`, read as const-global knowledge); `free`, `resize` and `remap` of the stock arena (x86_64-linux, aarch64-macos) and of a patched arena are proved against `FAllocSpec`; the stock arena satisfies `FAllocSpec`'s `alloc` for no child (obstructions O-A and O-E, kernel-checked; upstream patch draft). `deinit` and `reset(.free_all)` of the stock and the patched arena are proved against the reset contract (`reset_free_all_spec`: from the arena's state and the whole epoch token to the next epoch's, every node back with the child), and the retaining modes preserve the same ownership contract (`reset_retain_capacity_spec`, `reset_retain_with_limit_spec`: the postcondition of `reset_free_all_spec`, for every child that takes requests up to `2 ^ 63` bytes; the internal `loop66_ct` gives every node back to the child except at most one per list, kept as it is, resized in place or replaced by the child; the retained capacity and its relation to the limit are NOT stated, as `Kept`'s `sz` is existential: open, engine-ledger row `arena-retained-capacity`) over `wpts` header words (a new node's header is only a `wpts` word, kernel-checked: `HeaderTagCX`; it has thread rules since the per-byte mixed-size retirement, MM-21, kernel-checked: `HeaderMixCX`). Remaining for milestone 2: `alloc` of the patched arena, and the capacity the retaining modes keep (`arena-retained-capacity`). Legacy retirement (L5b): the hand-written model's proof layer (`ZigLean/Sep/Owned.lean`, `ZigLean/Sep/ArenaClient.lean`) is retired (`docs/alloc-spec.md`, "Retired claims"), and M01's acceptance is stated over the translated arena in the full-state logic: a slice that the arena takes back lies in one of its used nodes, so a foreign or stale slice has no token and no triple (`Core.granted_in_node`; natively `foreign_free_panics`), and `reset(.free_all)` keeps every other resource of the caller and ends with no grant (`reset_free_all_frame`). Pending: the production-style client, as one generic session client over `FAllocSpec` and a reset ledger, with instances for the `FixedBufferAllocator` (after its reset ledger) and the patched arena (after its `alloc`); the stock arena meets `FAllocSpec`'s `alloc` for no child (`not_fallocSpec_arena`). The translated arena's executable regressions run against its native results (`tests/roadmap/alloc-arena`, `AllocArena/Eval.lean`).
---
allocator identity, allocation ownership and qualified policies for arenas, fixed buffers and custom allocators. Preserve their actual free/reset behavior. Acceptance: cross-allocator frees are checked where invalid; arena reset invalidates exactly its blocks; a production-style allocator client has a proof.
