title: Sentinel and lower-level allocator APIs
status: partial
row: [PR102](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/102) merged bounded sentinel allocation. [PR124](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/124): `realloc` of alignment-1 nonsentinel `[]u8` (Zig 0.16.0) is recognized and modelled; `Triple.reallocSentinel` keeps the sentinel invariant on success and failure for every policy and remap mode, with an append client; raw vtable alloc/resize/remap/free contracts require power-of-two alignment, nonzero size and the whole live block. Remaining: other item types, alignments and versions of `realloc`; raw vtable calls are contracts only, not recognized by the translator. [PR139](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/139): under `--allocator-model translated` the raw vtable calls are translated (indirect calls through `mem.Allocator.VTable`), and `dispatch_allocSpec` lifts an allocator's `AllocSpec` to the dispatched vtable. Legacy retirement (L5a): the hand-written model's proof layer (`ZigLean/Sep/{Sentinel,SentinelRealloc,RawAlloc,Remap}.lean`) is retired (`docs/alloc-spec.md`, "Retired claims"), and the sentinel invariant is restated over the translated code for every allocator: `Interfaces.SentinelSpec` (`tests/roadmap/interfaces`) proves `make` (`allocSentinel`), `append` and `resize` (a `realloc` of the absorbed `len + 1`-byte buffer, then the sentinel store) and `release` of `tests/roadmap/sentinel-realloc/source.zig` from `CAllocSpec` of the environment's allocator with `fsym`, on Zig 0.17.0, 0.16.0, 0.15.2 and 0.14.1: success is a grant of the new length plus the sentinel byte with the retained bytes and the sentinel last, failure is `OutOfMemory` with the old grant unchanged. The raw vtable preconditions are `FAllocSpec`'s (`0 < len`, `k < 64`; `mem.Alignment` is a log2), proved for each translated allocator. The `std`-mode executable regressions (`byte-sentinel`, `sentinel-realloc`, `resize-remap`) keep testing `ZigLean/Mem/Alloc.lean`.
