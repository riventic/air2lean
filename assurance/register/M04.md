export required comptime parameters, extend selected allocator contracts and account for sentinel bytes during allocation, growth and free. Acceptance: sentinel invariants hold on success and failure; supported raw allocation APIs have alignment and size preconditions.

Bounded progress ([PR124](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/124)): `realloc` of alignment-1 nonsentinel `[]u8` (Zig 0.16.0) is recognized and modelled in the hand-written model (its proof layer is retired, below). [PR139](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/139): under `--allocator-model translated` the raw vtable calls are translated (indirect calls through `mem.Allocator.VTable`), and `dispatch_allocSpec` lifts an allocator's `AllocSpec` to the dispatched vtable. Legacy retirement (L5a): the hand-written model's proof layer (`ZigLean/Sep/{Sentinel,SentinelRealloc,RawAlloc,Remap}.lean`) is retired (`docs/alloc-spec.md`, "Retired claims"), and the sentinel invariant is restated over the translated code for every allocator: `Interfaces.SentinelSpec` (`tests/roadmap/interfaces`) proves `make` (`allocSentinel`), `append` and `resize` (a `realloc` of the absorbed `len + 1`-byte buffer, then the sentinel store) and `release` of `tests/roadmap/sentinel-realloc/source.zig` from `CAllocSpec` of the environment's allocator with `fsym`, on Zig 0.17.1, 0.16.0, 0.15.2 and 0.14.1: success is a grant of the new length plus the sentinel byte with the retained bytes and the sentinel last, failure is `OutOfMemory` with the old grant unchanged. The raw vtable preconditions are `FAllocSpec`'s (`0 < len`, `k < 64`; `mem.Alignment` is a log2), proved for each translated allocator. The `std`-mode executable regressions (`byte-sentinel`, `sentinel-realloc`, `resize-remap`) keep testing `ZigLean/Mem/Alloc.lean`.

Close-out review (2026-11-26): the acceptance holds for byte buffers. `Interfaces.SentinelSpec` (`make_spec`, `append_grant`, `resize_grant`, `release_spec`) elaborates on every version's translation and, on 0.17.1, with only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` (`interfaces/check.sh`); `FAllocSpec` (`ZigLean/Sep/Full/AllocSpec.lean`) gives `alloc`, `resize`, `remap` and `free` their `0 < len` and `k < 64` preconditions; the translated-path mutants are killed. Remaining then: a sentinel buffer of a wider item type (for example `[:0]u32`), whose byte count `(n + 1)·@sizeOf(T)` and alignment differ from the byte case.

Wider item types (lane m04-next, 2026-11-26): `Interfaces.WideSpec` (`tests/roadmap/interfaces`, program `wide.zig`) closes that gap. The sentinel invariant is stated once for every item size, alignment and sentinel bytes and proved for the `u16`, `u32` and `u64` copies of the generated code, for every allocator that meets `CAllocSpec`: on success a grant of `(n + 1)·@sizeOf(T)` bytes at `@alignOf(T)` with the retained items and the sentinel item last, on failure (allocator failure or a byte count that overflows `usize`) `OutOfMemory` with the old grant unchanged; `release` gives the whole grant back. It elaborates on the 0.17.1, 0.16.0, 0.15.2 and 0.14.1 translations, on 0.17.1 with only the standard axioms, and five mutants of the translated wrappers (bytes counted as items in `alloc`, `realloc` and `free`; alignment 1; the sentinel at byte `n`) are killed. The alignment and size preconditions at the vtable are `FAllocSpec`'s for every `k < 64`, here used at `k` = 1, 2 and 3. Scope: integer items of 1, 2, 4 and 8 bytes with sentinel `0`; partial correctness, as `CAllocSpec`.
