export required comptime parameters, extend selected allocator contracts and account for sentinel bytes during allocation, growth and free. Acceptance: sentinel invariants hold on success and failure; supported raw allocation APIs have alignment and size preconditions.

Bounded progress ([PR124](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/124)): `realloc` of alignment-1 nonsentinel `[]u8` (Zig 0.16.0) is recognized and modelled in the hand-written model (its proof layer is retired, below). [PR139](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/139): under `--allocator-model translated` the raw vtable calls are translated (indirect calls through `mem.Allocator.VTable`), and `dispatch_allocSpec` lifts an allocator's `AllocSpec` to the dispatched vtable. Legacy retirement (L5a): the hand-written model's proof layer (`ZigLean/Sep/{Sentinel,SentinelRealloc,RawAlloc,Remap}.lean`) is retired (`docs/alloc-spec.md`, "Retired claims"), and the sentinel invariant is restated over the translated code for every allocator: `Interfaces.SentinelSpec` (`tests/roadmap/interfaces`) proves `make` (`allocSentinel`), `append` and `resize` (a `realloc` of the absorbed `len + 1`-byte buffer, then the sentinel store) and `release` of `tests/roadmap/sentinel-realloc/source.zig` from `CAllocSpec` of the environment's allocator with `fsym`, on Zig 0.17.1, 0.16.0, 0.15.2 and 0.14.1: success is a grant of the new length plus the sentinel byte with the retained bytes and the sentinel last, failure is `OutOfMemory` with the old grant unchanged. The `std`-mode executable regressions (`byte-sentinel`, `sentinel-realloc`, `resize-remap`) keep testing `ZigLean/Mem/Alloc.lean`.

Close-out review (2026-11-26): the acceptance holds for byte buffers. `Interfaces.SentinelSpec` (`make_spec`, `append_grant`, `resize_grant`, `release_spec`) elaborates on every version's translation and, on 0.17.1, with only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` (`interfaces/check.sh`); `FAllocSpec` (`ZigLean/Sep/Full/AllocSpec.lean`) gives `alloc`, `resize`, `remap` and `free` their `0 < len` and `k < 64` preconditions; the translated-path mutants are killed. Remaining then: a sentinel buffer of a wider item type (for example `[:0]u32`), whose byte count `(n + 1)·@sizeOf(T)` and alignment differ from the byte case.

Wider item types (lane m04-next, 2026-11-26): `Interfaces.WideSpec` (`tests/roadmap/interfaces`, program `wide.zig`) closes that gap. The sentinel invariant is defined once for every item size, alignment and sentinel bytes and proved for the `u16`, `u32` and `u64` copies of the generated code, for every allocator that meets `CAllocSpec`: on success a grant of `(n + 1)·@sizeOf(T)` bytes at `@alignOf(T)` with the retained items and the sentinel item last, on failure (allocator failure or a byte count that overflows `usize`) `OutOfMemory` with the old grant unchanged; `release` gives the whole grant back. It elaborates on the 0.17.1, 0.16.0, 0.15.2 and 0.14.1 translations, on 0.17.1 with only the standard axioms, and five mutants of the translated wrappers (bytes counted as items in `alloc`, `realloc` and `free`; alignment 1; the sentinel at byte `n`) are killed. The alignment and size preconditions at the vtable are `FAllocSpec`'s for every `k < 64`, here used at `k` = 1, 2 and 3.

Versions: every theorem above elaborates on the 0.17.1, 0.16.0, 0.15.2 and 0.14.1 translations (`interfaces/check.sh`); the axioms are printed on 0.17.1 only, and the real-allocator witness is the 0.17.1 `FixedBufferAllocator`. Probes of this review (scratch programs exported with the patched compilers and translated with `registry-alloc.json`, `--allocator-model translated`; not committed): `rawAlloc`/`rawResize`/`rawRemap`/`rawFree`, the in-place `resize` and `remap` of a `[]u32`, `allocSentinel` with sentinel `0xff`, of `?*u8` with `null` and of `f32` translate on all four versions; `allocWithOptions(u16, n, .@"16", 0)` on 0.17.1, 0.16.0 and 0.15.2 (0.14.1 has another signature); `dupeZ` on 0.16.0, 0.15.2 and 0.14.1 and `dupeSentinel` on 0.17.1; `realloc` of a `[:0]u8` on 0.17.1, 0.15.2 and 0.14.1 (0.16.0: compile error); `allocSentinel(u0, n, 0)` on 0.17.1 and 0.14.1 (the patched 0.16.0 and 0.15.2 compilers crash in `allocWithOptionsRetAddr`, with and without the export; not compared with the stock compilers). A struct cannot be a sentinel item (Zig: "non-scalar sentinel type"), so structs are out of scope. The translations were not elaborated.

Remaining (owner of each: the allocator lane; none is blocked on another lane or on a decision):

1. Growth of the sentinel slice itself. Prove `realloc` (and `remap`, `resize`) of a `[:s]T` over the translated std code of 0.17.1 (`mem.absorbSentinel`), 0.15.2 and 0.14.1 from `CAllocSpec`, success and failure, with a mutant of the std sentinel accounting; 0.16.0 rejects the call at compile time and keeps the absorbed-buffer clients.
2. Lower-level entry points. A client of `rawAlloc`, `rawResize`, `rawRemap`, `rawFree` and of the in-place `resize`, proved from `CAllocSpec` on all four versions with the preconditions as its premises, and a check that a call outside them (length 0, another alignment or length than the grant's) has no proof.
3. Item kinds. `u0`: either proved or recorded as out of scope with the compiler crash on 0.16.0 and 0.15.2 as a row of the upstream table. Done (lane m04-rest, 2026-11-26): the statements and the theorems of `append` and `resize` are generic in the library (`ZigLean/Sep/Full/SentinelBuf.lean`), and `SentinelSpec` (`u8`) is the instance `sz = 1`, `k = 0`; `make` and `release` have no step between an instance's walk and the statement, so they have no generic theorem. Zig compiles one copy of a client per item type, so each instance still walks its own code (`fsym`). `Interfaces.KindsSpec` (program `kinds.zig`, all four versions) has the instances for a non-zero sentinel (`[:0xff]u8`), an optional pointer item (`[:null]?*u8`), a float item (`[:0]f32`) and an over-aligned slice (`[:0]align(16) u16`, `allocWithOptions`), and `copy_spec` for `dupeSentinel` (0.17.1) and `dupeZ` (older) of a nonempty `[]const u8` (generic `SentinelBuf.copy_grant`). A float has no `LawfulEnc` instance (a NaN's bytes are undefined), so the float walks name the value-wise region rules `regionStoreEnc`/`regionLoadEnc` (`ZigLean/Sep/Full/RegionEnc.lean`); the float item stored by `append` is any value, the sentinel that is read back is `+0.0`. Not covered: a copy of an empty slice (the `@memcpy` aliasing check needs the source's block), a float sentinel that is a NaN (its load is `.unspecified` in the model), and no mutant is designated for these instances.
4. Real allocator. `env_runs`/`fba_spec` for `WideSpec.env` and non-vacuity witnesses for `append`, `resize`, `release` and one `WideSpec` instance per alignment over the translated `FixedBufferAllocator` (`tests/roadmap/lists-fba`).
5. Mutants and negative tests. A mutant of the failure path (for example `reallocAdvanced` freeing the old buffer before the new allocation fails), one of the `u8` `realloc` path and one of a raw precondition; replace the three hand-model negative tests of M04 in `assurance/mutation-map.json` (`byte-sentinel/Check.lean`, two of `sentinel-realloc/Check.lean`) by checks of the translated path.
