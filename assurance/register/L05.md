[PR125](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/125): stored C/allowzero pointers use the storage dictionary `Zig.nullablePtrEnc` (null is eight zero bytes; zero bytes read back as null, other integer or undefined bytes are `.unspecified`), also as extern/auto struct fields and array items; projections from a C/allowzero base (`struct_field_ptr`, `ptr_elem_ptr`, `ptr_add`, `ptr_sub`) use the checked pointer formation `ptrProject` of every derived pointer (MM-3: offset 0 is defined, a nonzero offset from address zero or another provenance-free address is `.illegal`; `ptrProjectNonnull` also rejects address zero for a nonnullable result) and keep the base's provenance; `[*c]T`/`*allowzero T` convert to and from `?*T`/`?[*]T` by explicit null mapping. Proof-only `ZigLean.Mem.NullLemmas` (lawful storage, null round trip, projected-access block); twelve hand-written AIR cases. [PR159](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/159): fresh exports of the L05 fixture by 0.14.1–0.17.0 (`tests/roadmap/null-pointers/air`, `air-reject`, provenance) translate, and their model runs (`expected/<version>.txt`) equal the native runs on x86_64-linux and aarch64-linux (all four versions) and aarch64-macos (0.15.2–0.17.0); Zig 0.17.0 checks `&p.*.f` of a null C pointer and panics, as the model does from its AIR. Address-zero projection policy (`docs/null-pointers.md`): offset 0 into a nullable result is address zero; offset 0 into the nonnullable `*F` that 0.14.1/0.15.2 give `&p.*.f` and every nonzero offset are `.illegal`, the language's and the backend's verdict (a zero non-`allowzero` pointer; `getelementptr inbounds` poison), recorded as pinned illegal-behaviour exclusions in native diffs, not as over-approximations. Batch H (`codex/roadmap-batch-h`) completes the remaining scope ([null-pointers.md](docs/null-pointers.md)): optionals of nullable pointers (`?*allowzero T`, `?[*c]T`) are `Option Zig.Ptr` with a flag after the payload (`Zig.Enc.optionWith Zig.nullablePtrEnc`), so `none` differs from `some` of address zero; C/allowzero pointers in unions, tuples and error-union payloads use the null-byte dictionary; nullable slices (`[]allowzero T`, `Zig.nullableSliceEnc`) and C-pointer slicing, whose start projection is formed after Sema's null check (native: a panic, also with a nonzero start); `@memset`/`@memcpy` of zero items at address zero touch nothing and a positive count is `.illegal` (native: a fault); `@fieldParentPtr` through `*allowzero F` is pointer formation (a nonzero field offset from address zero is `.illegal`, `sub nuw` wraps); nonzero offsets from a provenance-free address outside a declared `--device-contract` window are `.illegal` under premise SEM-02 (no allocated object outside the model's blocks and the declared window), with a native witness (`mmioIsNull`). Fresh exports of 47 roots by 0.14.1–0.17.0 equal native runs (43 lines incl. four panic/fault `case` runs) on x86_64-linux and aarch64-linux (all four versions) and aarch64-macos (0.15.2–0.17.0); thirty hand-written AIR cases; 0.14.1's `memcpyLenMismatch` panic is mapped. Casts to or from an optional of a nullable pointer are rejected (Zig 0.16.0/0.17.0 miscompile them, [upstream draft](docs/upstream/optional-nullable-casts.md)). Residual fail-closed boundaries outside L05: nullable bit-pointers and volatile nullable pointers (L13), packed aggregates of pointers, tuples in memory, 32-bit profiles (T02).
