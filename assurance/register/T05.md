title: Native and WASM correspondence
status: research
row: Design and recommendation in [docs/research/T05.md](docs/research/T05.md): per-profile proofs, with a classified observation relation as qualification evidence. `tests/roadmap/wasm-correspondence` builds one source (DES `addDuration`/event-order/heap-queue kernels, integer kernels, the T02 `byteCount`/`succ` functions, float kernels) with a stock compiler as a native shared library and as wasm32-freestanding (Node); `scripts/wasm-correspondence.py check` (CI) relates every recorded pair by class: exact (word or panic), tolerated (floats: any NaN with any NaN, declared ulp bound for `@sin`) or target-specific (each side against its profile's `bits` model), excludes undetected illegal behaviour, and fails closed on unclassified exports, stale or missing records, unrelated pairs and a claim citing a theorem about another target's generated module (wasm32 claims cite `byteCount_w32`/`succ_w32`). Records: 0.17.0 and 0.16.0 on x86_64-linux, aarch64-macos and wasm32; 0.15.2 and 0.14.1 on x86_64-linux and wasm32; CI re-observes 0.16.0. Findings: ReleaseSafe 0.15.2–0.17.0 does not detect `@intFromFloat` of a NaN (x86_64 `minInt`, aarch64/wasm32 0; the model already says `.illegal`), and 0.14.1 wasm32 does not detect `@intFromFloat(2^63)`. Remaining: target-independent contracts proved once over the width-parameterized model for the DES kernels, a wasm32 translation of the case study, wasm32-wasi, threads and whole-program correspondence; the relation is bounded evidence, not a proof.
