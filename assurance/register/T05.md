id: T05
title: Native and WASM correspondence
status: research
row: Design and recommendation in [docs/research/T05.md](docs/research/T05.md): per-profile proofs, with a classified observation relation as qualification evidence. `tests/roadmap/wasm-correspondence` builds one source (DES `addDuration`/event-order/heap-queue kernels, integer kernels, the T02 `byteCount`/`succ` functions, float kernels) with a stock compiler as a native shared library, as wasm32-freestanding (Node) and as wasm32-wasi (a reactor under the WASI preview1 of the Node release that `relation.json` pins); `scripts/wasm-correspondence.py check` (CI) relates every recorded pair by class: exact (word or panic), tolerated (floats: any NaN with any NaN, declared ulp bound for `@sin`) or target-specific (each side against its profile's `bits` model), excludes undetected illegal behaviour, and fails closed on unclassified exports, stale or missing records, unrelated pairs and a claim citing a theorem about another target's generated module (wasm32 claims cite `byteCount_w32`/`succ_w32`). Records: 0.17.1 and 0.16.0 on x86_64-linux, aarch64-macos, wasm32-freestanding and wasm32-wasi; 0.15.2 and 0.14.1 on x86_64-linux and both wasm32 targets; CI re-observes 0.16.0 (not wasm32-wasi). The same source is exported by Zig 0.17.1 and 0.16.0 for x86_64-linux, aarch64-macos, wasm32-freestanding and wasm32-wasi (`exports.json`) and translated for each (`check.sh`): the checker rejects no construct of it on wasm32 (nor of the Flow `addDuration` wrapper). `WasmCorrespondence/Contracts.lean` states the contract of `des_time_add32`/`des_time_add64`, `des_event_before` and `usize_bits` once for every pointer width (`Kernels.Meets k w`) and proves once that translations meeting it agree on the exact kernels whatever their targets (`exact_agree`) and on `usize_bits` exactly when their widths agree; `V0_17_1/Proofs.lean` (and its 0.16.0 instance) proves it for the four translations with one proof per kernel (`meets`), the per-target statements are instances, and the relation's claims cite them (16 claims); the arithmetic is the width-generic `Zig.addWithOverflow_max_cases` (`ZigLean/Mem/WidthLemmas.lean`), of which the Flow case study's `timestamp32_body`/`timestamp64_body` are now instances (statements unchanged). Findings: ReleaseSafe 0.15.2–0.17.1 does not detect `@intFromFloat` of a NaN (x86_64 `minInt`, aarch64/wasm32 0; the model already says `.illegal`), and 0.14.1 wasm32 does not detect `@intFromFloat(2^63)`. Remaining: the same one-statement form for `pw_byte_count`/`pw_succ` (proved per target in `PointerWidth/Proofs.lean` today), the heap queue `des_queue_drain` (an `FTriple` with loop invariants per target) and the integer and float kernels; wasm32-wasi system calls, threads and whole-program correspondence; the relation is bounded evidence, not a proof.
