[PR680](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/680) (stage 0 complete): `Raw.canonicalizeTraced` records every Canon rewrite that changed a function, and each certificate report lists every function's `trusted-Canon` rewrites (the 23 certified functions trust `renumber`, 3 of them also `forwardReadOnlyCopies`); over the 3,491 corpus functions `renumber` fires on 3,394, `forwardReadOnlyCopies` on 1,014, `versionTags` on 434, `itemReads` on 122, `argRanks` on 106, `dropTrueChecks` on 57, `errTraces` on 24, `sinkPastChecks` on 12, the dead-code passes on 14. [PR695](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/695) (stage 2, `renumber`): `Renumber.execFunc_rename` proves once that every injective renaming of instruction IDs keeps `Sem.execFunc` (value and full memory) under every call oracle that keeps the register-cell contract `Oracle.CellFree`, which the program's own `run`/`runOn` keep (`run_cellFree`, `runOn_cellFree`); the certificate path binds each function natively (`CanonCertificate.renumberBound`: `renFunc` of `renumber`'s decoded input equals the canonical function under an injective `zipRen`) and drops a bound `renumber` from its `trusted-Canon` line, so in the 13 examples no function trusts `renumber` (97 trust no rewrite, 20 of them certified); the binding is per function inside the canonical program, and the program-level statement (tick policies invariant under renaming) is open. [PR129](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/129): the AIR certificates relate each emitted definition in the V01 fragment to the semantics of its canonical AIR (equality via fixpoint induction for recursive cliques). codex/v01-air-semantics: certificates are generated and kernel-checked for every example whose Gen.lean is its golden translation (13 examples, `Proofs/<Ex>/AirCert.lean`); 23 of their 124 functions certify, including memory functions over the same `Zig.MemM` (equality of value and memory), `pointers.sumTo` (register and escaping locals, a loop, a call), `basic.sum` and `options.find` (a pure function's slice parameter, at the call boundary through a view: values and errors equal, memories equal up to read entries) and `errors.parseDigit`/`errors.digitOrZero` (error unions); the Zig 0.15.2 slices goldens certify 6 more functions with pointer arithmetic and item loads in the test suite; every other function is listed with a reason. Remaining: canonicalization other than `renumber` is not proved (read-only-copy forwarding needs a placement-prophecy statement, docs/air-semantics.md), callers of view functions and view functions that call are excluded, memory callers get soundness only, and recursive memory cliques are excluded (stack budget).
