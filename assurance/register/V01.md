[PR129](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/129): first verified slice (scalar fragment, direct and mutual recursion, 9 functions). codex/v01-air-semantics: docs/air-semantics.md is the design (definitional interpreter over the decoded `Func` and `Zig.MemM`; per-family emission certificates; Canon preservation statement; register locals; loop commutation; SEM-06 shrink path); the semantics gains a typed SSA environment, memory through plain pointers (load/store of integers, `bool` and pointers, `struct_field_ptr` as `Zig.ptrProject`, pointer casts and comparisons), pointer arithmetic and slices of functions that use memory, register locals (an `alloc` whose address is never taken is a frame cell; a memory extension fails under ZigLean's placement, kernel-checked counterexample in docs/air-semantics.md §Locals) and loops (`execLoop`, `loop_comm`, `loop_comm_pure`, well-formed exits); slices of pure functions certify at the call boundary through a view (`SemView.execFunc_view`, memories equal up to read entries); optionals and error unions as values and `try`; 23 of 124 committed-example functions certify. Remaining: aggregates, enums, unions, optionals in memory, floats, vectors, globals, atomics, threads, external models and indirect calls are outside the semantics.
