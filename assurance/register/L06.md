[PR135](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/135): fresh 0.15.2 and 0.14.1 exports (`air-fresh/<version>`, with `provenance.json`) and a 0.16.0 re-export with the tree-built compiler (`export.sh --check` verifies the recorded compiler hashes); legacy schema-11 and unverified build profiles fail closed on the LLVM `eu_payload` shape (`mayMisplaceEuPayload`, `BuildProfile.unverified`); `test_cli.py` runs all three versions and the legacy schema-11 checks. [PR159](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/159): union-member bases (`ZigLean/Mem/ConstPtr.lean` `Proj.unionPayload`; the exporter resolves an `auto` union member at its payload offset when the compiler's equals the model's, else `union_field`; `tests/roadmap/union-bases`, exports of 0.14.1–0.17.0, proofs, native runs on stage2_x86_64 and LLVM); LLVM correspondence for legacy-schema and `stage2_llvm` inputs: `const-bases/llvm_correspondence.zig` compares six constant bases with their run-time projections natively (`-fllvm`/`-fno-llvm`, 0.14.1–0.17.0, x86_64-linux and aarch64-linux), only the alignment-1 `eu_payload` base differs and only on LLVM, and the translator accepts exactly the agreeing bases on the LLVM exports of every version and on the same files downgraded to schema 11; `const_bases.zig` runs natively on all four versions. Scope: legacy schema-11 inputs stay `unverified` (treated like LLVM).
