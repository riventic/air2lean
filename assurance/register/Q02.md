The checker proves designated Lean/Zig mutants exist, not that they are killed. [PR128](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/128): assurance/mutation-kills.json records which regression (differential example or proof module) killed each mutate.sh mutant, bound to the mutation's block hash; all 33 mutate.sh mutants are killed (d/i on the linux/amd64 reference host); `check` fails a designated mutant without a current kill and CI shards `verify` their logs; mutate.sh aborts when a mutation changes no source; new negative tests for C07/C12/C14/L09/L12/L14. Python-side mutants now also cover the Q01 generator oracle, Q03 schedule-cap accounting, Q06 perf gate, Q07 upgrade record, V03 AIR validator and trust report, T04 ABI probe and I08 safe publish (negative tests added for the atomic no-clobber race and an evidence-free qualification result). Fixture-local kills (this PR): `assurance/mutation-kills.json` also binds a `check` kill to each designated mutant that lives in a fixture file (`path#name`, the sha256 of the mutant file; `scripts/mutation_kill_log.py` is the runners' one logging call; `scripts/mutation-map.py kills record --log` records, never by hand), and `check` fails a designated fixture mutant without a current kill or whose file changed since; the fixture-local mutants of the earlier PRs now log their kills in their CI checks (`byte-permutation` and `error-storage` steps ran in no CI job before and were added). New designated mutants and negative tests close the gaps of F04 (mutate.sh `ag`/`ah`, killed by `Proofs.Floats.AllowedNeg`), M04 (translated sentinel allocation: `sentinel-alloc-omits-extra-byte`, `sentinel-stored-at-item-0`, run by `interfaces/check.sh`), P07 (`fingerprint-*` and `sidecar-runtime-revision-unchecked`), P03 (`queue-overflow-premise-dropped`, `queue-sum-off-by-one`) and T03 (byte-order mutants of the 0.17.1 s390x translation, killed by `BigEndian.Proofs` failing under `scripts/fixtures.py mutant`). The five `resize-remap` mutants only write patches of the retiring hand model `ZigLean/Mem/Alloc.lean` that no CI check applies: their kills stay `unrecorded` (reported as kill gaps, not designated for a complete row). Fixture-local Lean mutants of the retained translations close the gaps of T02 (`tests/roadmap/pointer-width/mutations.py`: 64-bit/32-bit layout swapped, `usize` add wrapping, inverted byte-count overflow flag, bounds check off by one, allocation item size; `PointerWidth.Proofs` must fail in the theorems of the changed fact), C08 (`tests/roadmap/futures/mutations.py`: wrong task result, dropped second-await result, lost capture write, swallowed error, cancel as await, skipped cancelation point, leaked future storage; each rejected by the runtime schedules with the named `require` message or by `Futures.Proofs` in `awaitValue_wp`) and E03 (`tests/roadmap/env-boundaries/mutations.py`: five contract-violating instances of the scripted client, five changed translations of std write/read/close, each rejected in the named theorems of `WriteAll.lean`/`StdIo.lean`); `scripts/lean_mutant.py` accepts a kill only when Lean fails with every error inside the checked file and in the expected declarations. Gaps closed for T05 (Python-side mutants of `scripts/wasm-correspondence.py`: a target-specific kernel checked against the native width on both sides, a one-sided NaN or an out-of-bound ulp distance tolerated, a stale record compared), X01 (of the C front-end record `tests/roadmap/c-frontend/run.py`: a file with a demoted function or a deferred Lean run counted as checked, a native mismatch ungated), M01 (the translated allocators' existing mutants now report their kills: a `FixedBufferAllocator` alloc that keeps `end_index`, rejected by `FBA.fallocSpec`, and a `PageAllocator` shrink that keeps the cut pages mapped, rejected by `PageSpec`) and P09 (the 19 seeded wrong invariants, measures and credit counts of `tests/roadmap/loop-tactics/{counted,nested}/mutants.json`, each rejected inside its named theorems by `invgen_mutants.py`, which now reports the kills). Gaps closed for L07 (the ten wrong-model results of `tests/roadmap/packed-casts/expected/wrong.txt`: a sentinel left out of the bits, a forced result sentinel, an array of `void` with bits, a float array and a packed union with swapped bytes; `check.sh` puts each in place of the model's line and reports the kill when the comparison with the retained native observation fails) and for the research rows that have code to mutate: C10 (`tests/roadmap/sc-order/check.sh`, a new CI step, runs `Sc.lean` on the committed observations; `mutations.py` holds five wrong observations it must reject and six mutants of its SC oracle and observation check, killed by the ORD-03 `#guard`, by a committed observation or by a wrong observation the mutant accepts), F03 (Python-side mutants of the fail-closed gates: an inventory row of a `*_optimized` tag without the translator's rejection, a tested source that sets the optimized float mode), V01 (the 13 one-operator AIR mutants of `tests/roadmap/air-semantics/test_lean.py`, each rejected in its function's certificate theorem) and V02 (a wrong view key, a guessed motive order of a recursive clique, and the ten wrong canonical functions of `CanonBinding.lean` that the native debug-ID and `renumber` bindings must not bind); `test_lean.py` now names these mutants and reports their kills. For these four rows the mutants cover what exists (the SC oracle, the rejection gates, the interpreted AIR of the certified fragment, the proved Canon bindings), not the unimplemented semantics; the map's notes say which mutants are due with the implementation. Remaining: designated mutants for C13 and negative tests and designated mutants for F02. Both rows are designs without an implementation to mutate (C13: no `WeakFair`, wait token, rule or client, only the bounded countdown family whose negative facts are now mapped as its negative tests; F02: no `LibmContract` premise, instance or client), so their mutants are due with their first implementation slice. [PR909](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/909): tests/roadmap/air-semantics/mutations.py mutates the translator's own sources in a check tree: 105 one-arm changes of the interpreter Air2Lean/Sem.lean over every family of arms (V01), each killed by the named checks of SemArms.lean and 18 of them also by named theorems of a committed certificate, and one mutant per Canon rewrite without a preservation theorem (V02, 9), each killed by its check of CanonRewrites.lean; all kills are recorded. Not designated: the arms that Sem.lean's own theorems pin (block and loop exits, the argument check of `execFunc`, the `Value` accessors), where no mutant builds, and the free order of a frame's blocks, which the model does not observe. Also remaining: mutants beyond mutate.sh, the Python-side set and the fixture files.
