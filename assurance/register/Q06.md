title: Translation and proof performance budgets
status: partial
row: [PR116](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/116) merged: Real-module workload suite (7 uniform golden AIR sets with reference translations), air2lean --timing-json per-phase timing, cold/warm translate/elaborate/proof recorder with peak RSS and output hash, recorded Darwin arm64 baseline and regression gate. No measurement-driven optimization yet; budgets are reference-platform specific. [PR129](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/129): 18 workloads with recorded reference-platform budgets and per-module proof timings; a portable gate (generous `portable_max_*` limits on any runner, CI step) catches gross regressions and any emitted-Lean change; measured hotspots documented; two optimizations: Anon renumbering skips functions without marker instances (renumber phase sync 91→73 ms, threadsync 13.9→6.8 ms; 58 AIR dirs byte-identical) and two RwLock facts lifted to standalone lemmas (module 11.3→9.4 s, same theorems). [PR154](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/154): proof time is gated on any machine: `record` times a pure-Lean calibration workload (`tests/roadmap/perf-budgets/Calibration.lean`, about 5.8 s) and `gate --normalized` compares elaboration, proof builds and 6 to 8 hotspot proof modules per workload as multiples of it (`max_ratio`, `budget.modules`), failing closed without a matching calibration or per-module timings, with CI gating the `threadsync` workload on 0.16.0; the hotspot analysis found that `Threadsync.Handoff.enc_box` was an async-profiling artifact (0.4 s serially) and fixed the real `Threadsync.WaitGroup` one by the general lemma `Zig.Result.eq_pure_of_run` (module 19-22 s to 15-17 s, no statement changed); 18 workloads rebaselined with unchanged emitted Lean. Remaining: the kernel checking of the large `refine`-chain proofs in `Threadsync.Handoff` (17 s) and `WaitGroup` (9 s) is not reduced; the ratio assumes CPU-bound work scales uniformly across machines.
