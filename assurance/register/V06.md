title: Explicit outcome taxonomy

row: [PR96](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/96) merged with explicit outcomes and exclusions. [PR119](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/119): scripts/outcomes.py maps typed differential observations onto one taxonomy (valid, nondeterministic_valid, error_return, panic, illegal/unspecified, unsupported_semantics, deadlock, divergence/fuel, search_cap); claims.py check --diff and project.py coverage refuse no-panic/guaranteed-return absence claims whose evidence has a capped, fuel-bounded, unsupported or unspecified outcome or a denied failure, and error returns never refuse no-panic; a Lean fixture keeps error returns apart from model failures. [PR126](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/126): unsupported timers are their own outcome: `Zig.Error.unsupportedTimer` (generated `time.Timer.start`/`.read`, `Thread.Futex.timedWait`, and the timed scheduler's no-clock, wrong-clock and unselected-timeout paths) is `unspecified_timer` in outcomes, accounting and claims, refuses no-panic/guaranteed-return with a timer-specific reason, and `claims.py check --diff` accepts only summaries bound to the current tree. Batch A: the taxonomy is the vocabulary of contracts too: every manifest goal has an outcome contract (`goals[].outcome_contract`), by default values and error returns, or a declared `"outcomes": {"admits": [...]}` that the manifest loader refuses when it names an unknown or duplicate outcome, admits an outcome its own strength rules out, or admits an incomplete-evidence class (search cap, divergence, unspecified, unspecified timer, unsupported); sampled outcomes outside the contract refute it and block functional levels, incomplete evidence leaves it unassessed, evidence never proves it, and it is separate from correspondence. Theorem check: `tools/OutcomeContracts.lean` derives a conservative constructor upper bound from the audited kernel postcondition (including nested Option/Except ErrName wrappers); explicit narrower declarations fail closed, equal bounds are verified, wider declarations are reported as under-claims, and unsupported or opaque predicates cannot establish a contract. Claims and receipt coverage share the check. Partial postconditions retain unexcluded divergence/stack overflow; implicit defaults are labelled strength-relative rather than a proved complete outcome set. Real audited fixtures, receipt regressions and mutant `outcome-contract-ignores-theorem` cover the gate.
