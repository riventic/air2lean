id: C12
title: Memory-model adequacy
status: research
row: Design and recommendation in [docs/research/C12.md](docs/research/C12.md): a per-target correspondence argument now (DRF-SC for plain accesses, the RC11 mappings to x86-TSO and ARMv8 for atomics), with the model expansion (promises, SC order) as the long-term direction after C10. Premise [ORD-06](docs/premises.md#ord-06) (memory-model adequacy, trusted) is implied by THR-01 and ORD-01, so every concurrency theorem carries it (1647 of 3094 indexed); its qualification record (`memory_model` in `assurance/premises.json`) gives per target (x86_64-linux, aarch64-macos, aarch64-linux) the hardware model, the argument and evidence files, and lists wasm32/s390x as unqualified (they reject concurrency). `scripts/premises.py check`/`compiled` fail when a concurrency theorem or a catalogue report omits ORD-06 or an evidence file is missing; `scripts/claims.py` lists ORD-06 in each concurrency claim's `premises` from the audit's new `runtime_modules` and rejects a goal whose audit lacks them. Remaining: the argument is not proved (LLVM's optimizer is assumed C++11-sound, ORD-02 load buffering stays an assumption on ARMv8), the model expansion that would remove ORD-02/ORD-03, and a wasm32 entry before wasm32 threads.
---
establish a target/compiler correspondence argument for the supported subset, impose verified usage restrictions, or expand the model. Make the choice visible in proof claims. Acceptance: concurrency reports identify this premise and its qualification evidence. It must not disappear behind a general claim of race freedom.
