title: Memory-model adequacy
status: complete
row: Completed (2026-11-24, after the audit [docs/architecture-audit/memory-model-target-binding.md](docs/architecture-audit/memory-model-target-binding.md)): design and recommendation in [docs/research/C12.md](docs/research/C12.md), a per-target correspondence argument (DRF-SC for plain accesses, the RC11 mappings to x86-TSO and ARMv8 for atomics). Premise [ORD-06](docs/premises.md#ord-06) (memory-model adequacy, trusted) is implied by THR-01 and ORD-01, so every concurrency theorem carries it; its qualification record (`memory_model` in `assurance/premises.json`) gives per qualified target (x86_64-linux, aarch64-macos, aarch64-linux) the hardware model, the argument and evidence files. `scripts/premises.py check`/`compiled` fail when a concurrency theorem or a catalogue report omits ORD-06, an evidence file is missing, or a theorem carries ORD-06 over a generated module whose profile header records a target without a qualification entry (target binding, general over the `target_qualified` records); `scripts/claims.py` lists ORD-06 in each concurrency claim's `premises` from the audit's `runtime_modules`, rejects a goal whose audit lacks them and rejects a goal whose root translation records an unqualified target. Scope: the argument is trusted, not proved (LLVM's optimizer is assumed C++11-sound; ORD-02 load buffering is an assumption on ARMv8). s390x-linux and mips64-linux, whose translation admits atomics and threads (T03), and wasm32 are unqualified: no theorem or claim can carry ORD-06 for them. A module without a recorded target (PRF-01, PRF-03) carries ORD-06 for the qualified targets only. The source index binds the premises a theorem's own closure gives; 14 sequential theorems on s390x and wasm32 translations still list ORD-06 as a source-fallback upper bound. Follow-up: the model expansion that removes ORD-02/ORD-03 under C10; a wasm32 entry with wasm32 threads under T02; an s390x-linux or mips64-linux entry (hardware model, mapping argument, litmus runs on hardware; a maintainer decision) before a concurrency theorem on those targets.
---
establish a target/compiler correspondence argument for the supported subset, impose verified usage restrictions, or expand the model. Make the choice visible in proof claims. Acceptance: concurrency reports identify this premise and its qualification evidence. It must not disappear behind a general claim of race freedom.
