in-place success, relocated success and failure; specify preserved bytes, invalidated pointers and capacity changes. Acceptance: both successful growth paths and failure cleanup are proved and differentially tested with suitable allocators.

Bounded progress: [PR139](https://github.com/riventic/air2lean/pull/139): the translated `PageAllocator`'s `resize`/`remap` are proved from its code over the OS model: `mremap` grows in place when the grown pages are free, moves under `MAYMOVE` (copying the live bytes, ending the old mapping) or fails; a shrink unmaps the cut pages (`TotalTriple.mremapGrow`/`mremapShrink`, `TotalTriple.munmapTail`; a mutant that leaks them is rejected).

Native comparison of the proved paths (`tests/roadmap/alloc-translated`, [alloc-page.md](docs/alloc-page.md#growth-by-remap-and-realloc-m02)): `page.zig`'s `page_grow`, `page_grow_blocked` and `page_grow_stale` grow a block of the translated `PageAllocator` through `Allocator.remap` and `Allocator.realloc`. The model's lines and the native run's lines (stock Zig 0.16.0, `ReleaseSafe`) are compared with one file per target: x86_64-linux natively (`mremap`: grown in place, moved when a live neighbour is in the way, failed for a `2^47`-byte length; the old address faults after the move) and aarch64-macos (no `mremap`: `remap` fails past the page with the old block unchanged, `realloc` copies to a new mapping). No mismatch.

Against the acceptance sentence, for Zig 0.16.0 on x86_64-linux and aarch64-macos: the in-place growth, the relocated growth and the failure cleanup are proved (`PageSpec.remap_spec`, `resize_spec`, `PageSpecMacos`; the fixed buffer's `FBA.fallocSpec`) and compared with the native run of the page allocator; the std-mode byte buffer has its own native probe (`tests/roadmap/resize-remap`). Left inside the sentence:

- Zig 0.17.1: no translated `PageAllocator` of that version, so neither proof nor comparison (the 0.17.1 exporter writes `page.zig`'s AIR; it is not translated or proved yet).
- The fixed buffer's `remap` growth and `realloc` are proved but compared natively only through `resize` (`fba_resize`).
- Not forced natively: a kernel that moves a mapping with room in place, and a `mremap` that fails for lack of memory. The model runs both (its oracles); no native run does.

Beyond the sentence, recorded elsewhere: `realloc` of other element types and sentinel slices (the wrapper contracts of [alloc-spec.md](docs/alloc-spec.md), M04), custom allocator identity (M01) and address reuse (M05).
